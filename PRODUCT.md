# Scalpr Product Definition

## Mission

Scalpr is an execution-intelligence platform for disciplined trading decisions. It measures market, momentum, execution, and thesis quality; it does not claim to predict exact prices or replace the trader.

## Product principles

- Follow confirmed momentum rather than forecasting reversals.
- Add only when a winning thesis receives new evidence; never average into losers.
- Cut risk quickly and keep enforcement independent of AI interpretation.
- Express confidence, evidence, contradiction, missing data, and invalidation conditions.
- Prefer abstention to fabricated certainty.

## V2 initial product boundary

- Single-user, local-first application.
- SPY options, 0-2 DTE.
- Paper trading and shadow observation only.
- Human-initiated trades; AI is advisory.
- Preserve the dashboard, Standard ratchet Guard, workup, premarket/regime context, journal, and Wave/Incubation shadow workflows.

## V2 intelligence output

Each thesis evaluation should contain:

- Market state and data-quality status.
- Momentum and execution-quality assessments.
- Supporting and contradicting evidence.
- Hold/add/reduce/exit/wait posture as coaching, never an order.
- Invalidation and reevaluation conditions.
- Confidence with explicit missing inputs.

## Out of scope until separately approved

- Live-money execution.
- Autonomous entries or exits.
- Multi-user/customer platform behavior.
- Expansion beyond SPY and 0-2 DTE.
- Claims of calibrated probability or edge without a qualifying cohort.
